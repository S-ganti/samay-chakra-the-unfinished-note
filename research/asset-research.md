# Samay Chakra — environment source and layer research

Research date: 15 October 2026. The eight chapter names, times and raga associations below come from the user-supplied PDF as inspected by the lead agent. Lighting, asset combinations, parallax values and musical implementation below are proposed creative directions, not claims from that PDF. This document is a verified source shortlist; no downloaded asset is claimed to be integrated merely because it appears here.

## Visual direction

Build original Indian ring architecture, landscapes and everyday Bengaluru details with the spacious, contemplative presentation the user admires in GRIS. Nomada's own description emphasizes delicate art, animation, music and unhurried platform exploration; its official page supplies screenshots and the reveal trailer for reference. [Official GRIS page](https://nomada.studio/gris-game/)

The proposed synthesis is realistic material and lighting detail contained inside large, legible painted shapes: broad negative space around the ring, ink-like silhouettes, subdued distant detail, textured close foliage, and a consistent small traveller for scale. Do not assemble GRIS screenshots into the game. The architecture, traveller and compositions should be original to Samay Chakra.

## Layer and Blender production recipe

Proposed fixed-camera stack, far to near: `01_sky` (0.00 parallax), `02_far_landscape` (0.08), `03_far_architecture` (0.18), `04_canopy` (0.35), `05_hero_ring` (0.62), `06_play_plane` (1.00), `07_near_foliage` (1.12), `08_atmosphere` (separate depth bands). These are starting values to tune in the eventual engine.

Use an orthographic camera with the traveller and walking surfaces on one consistent plane. A main composition should support a short horizontal route, one optional upper ledge reached by double jump, and a broad flight arc toward the ring. Keep required traversal visible in the composition and avoid intricate maze-like branching.

Render each depth group with alpha and a little overscan so camera drift never exposes empty edges. Maintain common object origins and a fixed camera across passes. Keep contact shadows with the surfaces that receive them. Render particles, beams and foreground motion separately so a still background can feel alive without moving every painted element.

For refinement, save an EXR master with Combined, Mist, Normal and Cryptomatte passes. Mist supports controlled distance fading; Cryptomatte supports object/material isolation. Those features are described in Blender's official pass documentation. [Blender render passes](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/4.4/render/layers/passes.html)

Begin look development with AgX and moderate contrast, then grade each chapter as a palette rather than turning every light fully saturated. AgX's handling of bright saturated colors is documented by Blender; final exposure and colors still need visual review. [Blender AgX release notes](https://developer.blender.org/docs/release_notes/4.0/color_management/)

Use small texture maps while arranging: 1–2K for most materials and 1–2K HDRIs. Raise resolution only where close foreground renders reveal insufficient detail. Reduce or bake high-resolution model geometry before it enters a real-time prototype. For a painted finish, limit tiny material contrast at distance and overlay subtle original paper/granulation treatment after lighting; keep the highest contrast around the traveller and the chapter's ring.

## Completion checks for a polished art pass

- All eight scenes read as different times of day when viewed as small thumbnails.
- Every scene shares the ring language, scale cues and a coherent traveller silhouette.
- Traversable surfaces and flight openings remain legible in grayscale.
- Deep foreground detail does not obscure the walking line.
- Alpha layers have clean edges and sufficient overscan; movement reveals no gaps.
- Repeating models and texture tiling are disguised by authored placement, masks and varied silhouettes.
- Sun direction, cast shadows, illuminated mist and practical lights agree.
- Music changes with space and transitions without an abrupt loop seam.
- Final package records actual asset files, author, source URL, license, modifications and usage per scene.
